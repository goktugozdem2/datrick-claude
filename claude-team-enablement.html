Enablement outputs

Give the team a usable workflow and the evidence to govern it.

Decision

Workflow and baseline

A defined task family, current cost or delay, users, repositories, quality bar, exclusions, and measurable pilot outcome.

Controls

Access and usage boundary

Repository scope, data policy, secrets handling, tools, permissions, human approval, logging, incident response, and offboarding.

Quality

Evaluation and review

Representative tasks, deterministic checks, technical rubrics, failure categories, escalation, and evidence required before acceptance.

Adoption

Team playbook and decision

Training, reusable instructions, review conventions, office hours, feedback, adoption metrics, and a stop, improve, or expand recommendation.
