When to use it

Start here when the database is operating, but confidence is not.

Coverage

Monitoring exists without accountable response

Alerts, severity, service hours, escalation, incident ownership, and update expectations are incomplete or split across several teams.

Recovery

Backup success is standing in for restore evidence

Jobs appear green, but restore paths, dependencies, recovery timing, business validation, and drill ownership are not current.

Performance

Recurring slowness has no evidence-led backlog

Query behavior, resource saturation, blocking, growth, maintenance, and application boundaries are repeatedly rediscovered.

Ownership

One person or vendor holds critical context

Access, runbooks, dependencies, recurring work, known risks, repositories, and decision routes cannot be transferred cleanly.

Assessment outputs

Make the support decision from a documented baseline.

Baseline

Estate and responsibility map

In-scope platforms, environments, owners, authorized access paths, dependencies, coverage, and known gaps.

Risk

Prioritized operating register

Continuity, recovery, performance, capacity, access, change, and documentation risks separated by consequence and evidence.

Action

Immediate and 30-day actions

Containment, validation, remediation, monitoring, restore, documentation, and ownership work ordered by urgency.

Scope

Recommended service boundary

A bounded fix, handover recovery, or ongoing remote DBA model with explicit assumptions, exclusions, authority, and evidence.
