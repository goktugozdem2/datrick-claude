When teams bring us in

Managed DBA is most valuable when database ownership has become a business risk.

Coverage gap

Your team cannot justify another full-time specialist

Add senior DBA capacity for defined coverage windows, backlog, escalation and specialist work without creating another single point of failure.

Turn alerts into an operating model with severity, triage, escalation, evidence, ownership and follow-up.

Recovery risk
