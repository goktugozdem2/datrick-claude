Transition

Take over in phases without pretending undocumented work is understood.

  1. 1

    Scope the risk

    Confirm critical reporting products, users, deadlines, incidents, ownership gaps, current providers, coverage, and access constraints.

  2. 2

    Build the inventory

    Collect tenant, workspace, report, model, source, gateway, capacity, security, refresh, release, and business-owner evidence.

  3. 3

    Stabilize priority services

    Address broken ownership, stale credentials, recurring refresh failures, unsupported gateways, access exposure, and missing escalation routes.

  4. 4

    Agree the operating contract

    Document coverage, severity, response, responsibilities, approvals, reporting, exclusions, service measures, and acceptance.

  5. 5

    Run a controlled service

    Operate incidents, requests, releases, monitoring, stakeholder updates, documentation, and the improvement backlog through a named lead.

FAQ

Questions to resolve before selecting a Power BI managed-services provider.

What do Power BI managed services include?

The agreed service can include tenant and workspace governance, semantic-model ownership, refresh and gateway reliability, report support, access review, incident response, release coordination, performance analysis, documentation, service reporting, and a prioritized improvement backlog. Coverage and exclusions are documented before transition.

How much do Power BI managed services cost?

A scoped operating assessment starts from $7,500. Ongoing senior delivery starts from $15,000 per month. Final pricing depends on tenant size, number and criticality of reporting products, coverage window, incident responsibility, gateway and source complexity, change volume, documentation quality, and required ownership.

Can Datrick take over Power BI after a developer leaves?

Yes. The transition starts with an evidence-led inventory of workspaces, reports, semantic models, dataflows, sources, gateways, credentials, owners, refresh schedules, security, release paths, incidents, and business deadlines. Missing knowledge is recorded as risk rather than guessed.

Can Datrick work with our internal Power BI team?

Yes. Datrick can own a defined back line, reliability workstream, incident route, governance scope, release function, or improvement backlog while internal analysts and developers retain product ownership. Responsibilities and escalation boundaries are written into the operating model.

Do Power BI managed services also cover Microsoft Fabric?

They can. Fabric capacity, workspaces, pipelines, lakehouses, warehouses, semantic models, gateways, security, monitoring, and release dependencies can be included when they affect the reporting service. Review Datrick’s Microsoft Fabric managed services and support model for the wider operating scope. The contracted scope identifies which Fabric workloads Datrick operates and which remain with the client or another provider.

What evidence should a Power BI managed-services provider deliver?

Useful evidence includes a current asset and ownership inventory, service boundaries, freshness and incident measures, access and gateway records, release and rollback evidence, known risks, recurring-failure analysis, runbooks, status reporting, and a prioritized improvement backlog. A provider should distinguish verified facts from assumptions and recommendations.
