Claude Certified Associate - Foundations: exam format, domains, and study plan.

The entry credential for client-facing and delivery practitioners who need to use Claude effectively, evaluate its output, and integrate it responsibly into real work.

Certification overviewUpdated July 20268 min read

Reviewed against Anthropic's official Associate Foundations certification page by Can Goktug Ozdem. Last checked July 11, 2026.

Claude Certified Associate - Foundations validates practical use of Claude in everyday professional work. Anthropic lists prompting and task execution, output evaluation, product and model selection, workflow integration, Projects and knowledge sources, responsible use, and troubleshooting among the capabilities covered.

The exam is aimed at client-facing and delivery practitioners rather than engineers who build the underlying application. It fits consultants, analysts, project professionals, customer success teams, operations specialists, and other practitioners who need to produce reliable work with Claude and explain how that work was reviewed.

How to prepare for Claude Certified Associate - Foundations

Practice evaluation before prompt polishing

The largest domain is output evaluation and validation. Start by defining what a correct, useful, and safe answer looks like. Check Claude's output against the source, mark unsupported claims, identify omissions, and decide whether the result can be used directly, needs revision, or must be escalated. A strong prompt is not enough if the practitioner cannot recognize a weak answer.

Turn individual tasks into controlled workflows

Practice mapping a task from request to approved outcome. Specify the source material, prompt owner, reviewer, acceptance criteria, system of record, and final handoff. This makes workflow integration concrete and exposes where Claude should assist rather than decide.

Use context and Projects deliberately

Build a small Project with curated instructions and source files. Test what happens when sources conflict, a file becomes outdated, or the request falls outside the available knowledge. Good configuration reduces repeated prompting, but it does not remove the need to validate output.

Study responsible use through scenarios

Work through realistic cases involving confidential client data, personal information, legal or financial implications, biased source material, and high-impact recommendations. Be able to explain what can be entered, what must be redacted, who approves the output, and how limitations are communicated.

A two-week Associate Foundations study plan

  1. Days 1-2: Read the official exam guide and map every domain to one example from your own work.
  2. Days 3-4: Practice task prompts with explicit context, constraints, examples, output format, and acceptance criteria.
  3. Days 5-6: Evaluate ten Claude outputs against source material and document why each passes, needs revision, or fails.
  4. Days 7-8: Design two end-to-end workflows with ownership, review, escalation, and handoff.
  5. Days 9-10: Configure a Project and test conflicting, missing, and outdated knowledge.
  6. Days 11-12: Review product selection, responsible-use scenarios, and troubleshooting patterns.
  7. Days 13-14: Complete a timed knowledge review and revisit weak domains using practical exercises.

Associate Foundations versus Developer Foundations

Choose Associate Foundations if you primarily use Claude to deliver analysis, documents, research, recommendations, or operational work. Choose Developer Foundations if you build API integrations, tools, MCP servers, production applications, or agents. The first credential validates disciplined use; the second validates implementation responsibility.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the Claude Certified Associate - Foundations exam?

Anthropic's official certification page lists 60 multiple-choice and multiple-response questions with 120 minutes of exam time.

What is the passing score for Claude Certified Associate - Foundations?

The published passing score is 720 on a scaled range of 100 to 1,000.

How much does the Claude Certified Associate - Foundations exam cost?

The published exam price is $99. Claude Partner Network discounts may apply based on partner tier. Confirm the current price at registration.

How long is the Claude Certified Associate - Foundations credential valid?

Anthropic's official exam page states that the credential is valid for 12 months.

Who should take Claude Certified Associate - Foundations?

The exam is intended for client-facing and delivery practitioners who use Claude in everyday work, including prompting, evaluating and refining output, selecting products and models, integrating workflows, managing Projects and knowledge sources, applying responsible AI practices, and troubleshooting.

Does Claude Certified Associate - Foundations count toward Claude Partner Network tier eligibility?

No. Anthropic's official exam page states that this certification does not count toward Claude Partner Network tier eligibility.
