Partner fit

The model works when there is real client demand and a clear accountable partner.

Capacity

A client need has arrived before your capacity plan

Your team has the account and understands the business context, but it is full, missing a specialist, or unable to cover the delivery window. Datrick adds execution capacity against a defined scope instead of asking you to add permanent capacity ahead of uncertain demand.

Specialism

The work crosses data systems your core team does not own

A migration, production database, BI reliability problem, analytics backlog, or data pipeline requires focused experience. The partner remains accountable to the client while Datrick supplies the technical delivery layer.

Continuity

A key person left or refuses a usable handover

The immediate need is not simply more hands. It is controlled ownership recovery: inventory, access, dependencies, runbooks, risk, escalation, and a support model that protects the client relationship during the transition.

Expansion

One urgent request could become recurring work

A relationship may begin with DBA support, migration validation, or reporting recovery. If delivery quality is proven, the partner can add adjacent services or move to an ongoing program without changing who owns the client.

Qualification matters.Datrick is not a lead marketplace or anonymous staffing pool. The strongest fit is a professional services firm with an active client need, a responsible account owner, and authority to provide the required access.

Operating safeguards

The relationship should be protected by explicit controls, not goodwill alone.

Account

Your firm remains the commercial owner

You control client pricing, packaging, relationship decisions, and commercial commitments. Datrick contracts and scopes its delivery with the partner unless another structure is explicitly agreed.

Communication

Client contact follows an agreed model

Datrick can work only with your team, join as a named specialist, or operate under your brand. Meeting participation, email identity, reporting routes, and escalation contacts are established before client-facing work.

Confidentiality

Commercial and technical boundaries are documented

Confidentiality, non-solicitation expectations, branding, data handling, and permitted use of client information belong in the engagement terms. Sensitive credentials are handled through approved secure channels, never the website form.

Access

Least privilege and named authorization guide access

Production access depends on scope and approval. Named accounts, role boundaries, auditability, credential ownership, expiry, revocation, and emergency procedures are agreed with the authorized party.

Change control

Critical changes require evidence and approval

The operating plan defines who can request, review, approve, execute, validate, and roll back a change. Datrick does not treat partner urgency as permission to bypass client controls.

Continuity

Delivery should leave an operating record

Status notes, decisions, blockers, risks, runbooks, known issues, and handover artifacts reduce dependency on individual memory. The exact record depends on scope and the partner's delivery process.

Qualify the work before making a client promise.

  4. 4

    Establish authorized access, repositories, environments, stakeholders, documentation, issue tracking, and the first prioritized delivery backlog.

  5. 5

    Execute the agreed work, surface blockers and risk, record decisions, and provide reviewable outputs before critical client-facing changes.

  6. 6

    Continue or hand back

    Extend the scope, move into an ongoing program, or complete a documented handover based on demand, results, and partner preference.

Not the right fit

Clear exclusions protect both firms.

Unqualified

No defined client need or accountable buyer

Datrick does not reserve delivery capacity for speculative resale, generic lead generation, or an opportunity with no authorized decision maker and no describable technical need.

Unsafe

Pressure to bypass access or change controls

A partner request cannot substitute for client authorization. Work that requires hidden access, credential sharing through insecure channels, or unreviewed critical changes is not a fit.

Unbounded

Unlimited responsibility without scope or priority control

Ongoing support still needs coverage boundaries, service ownership, escalation definitions, workload assumptions, and a process for accepting new work.

Misrepresented

Claims or commitments Datrick has not reviewed

The partner should not promise named staffing, certifications, response times, outcomes, or delivery dates on Datrick's behalf before written confirmation.

Partner FAQ

Questions to settle before introducing a delivery partner.

Can Datrick provide white-label DBA services for an MSP?

Yes. Datrick can provide database operations, monitoring, incident support, backup and restore coordination, performance review, handover recovery, migration support, and documented escalation behind an MSP or IT service firm's client relationship. Identity, communication, access, change authority, service boundaries, and client-facing approval are agreed before delivery.

Does Datrick work directly with our client?

Only when the operating model requires it and the partner approves it. Datrick can work partner-only, join client meetings as a named specialist, or operate under the partner's brand. Communication, identity, escalation, and approval boundaries are agreed before delivery begins.

How does Datrick protect the partner's client relationship?

The partner retains account ownership, commercial control, and final client-facing approval. Confidentiality, non-solicitation expectations, communication channels, branding, access, and escalation paths are documented in the engagement terms rather than left as assumptions.

Can we set our own client pricing and margin?

Yes. Datrick scopes and prices its delivery relationship with the partner. The partner controls its own client pricing, packaging, and commercial terms. Datrick does not prescribe the partner's resale margin.

What information is needed to qualify a partner engagement?

The initial written intake should describe the client situation, service area, environment, expected workload, urgency, access constraints, decision makers, desired communication model, acceptance criteria, and commercial shape. Sensitive credentials or production secrets should not be sent through the website form.

Can Datrick provide a dedicated full-time team?

A dedicated or ongoing delivery model can be considered when the workload, responsibilities, communication cadence, security requirements, and minimum commitment are clear. Named staffing and availability are confirmed during scoping; they are not assumed before capacity review.

Which data services can be delivered under our brand?

Typical entry points include database operations and NOC support, database handover recovery, migration delivery and QA, BI reporting reliability, analytics, and data pipelines. Final scope depends on the environment, access, risk, and available specialists.
