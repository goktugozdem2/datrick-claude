Typical problems

PostgreSQL performance problems are usually multi-signal problems.

Slow SQL and unstable plans

Review plan shape, row estimates, scans, joins, sorts, parameter behavior and representative runtime.

Indexes, statistics and vacuum

Find missing, redundant or ineffective indexes; stale statistics; bloat; dead tuples; and maintenance gaps.

Locks and connection saturation

Correlate blocking, long transactions, pool pressure, concurrency and application retry behavior.

Replication and resource pressure

Separate transport/apply lag from CPU, memory, storage, WAL and workload bottlenecks.
