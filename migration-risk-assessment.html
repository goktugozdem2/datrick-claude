Common failure modes

The highest migration risks often sit outside the transfer job.

Scope

Source and target are defined, but business acceptance is not

Technical completion cannot prove that the target produces trusted reports, downstream feeds, customer outputs, or operational decisions.

Validation

Row counts pass while meaning changes

Aggregates, keys, relationships, transformations, timestamps, currencies, permissions, and exception handling need explicit reconciliation rules.

Cutover

A runbook exists without decision gates

Start, pause, rollback, and accept decisions lack named authority, measurable thresholds, timing, evidence, and stakeholder communication.

Handover

The project ends before the new environment can be operated

Monitoring, incidents, recurring work, access, backup and restore, known issues, documentation, and owners are incomplete at handover.
